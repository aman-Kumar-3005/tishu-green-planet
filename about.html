Our Story

Promoting Plant Love & Transforming Urban Spaces

What began on July 6, 2020 as an earnest desire to share plant care advice has blossomed into one of India’s most relatable, high-energy green living communities. Today, across Instagram (34.6K followers) and YouTube (35.4K subscribers), Tishu Green Planet connects with plant parents, hobbyists, nursery owners, and families looking for greener homes.

Through comedic reels, candid gardening tips (such as how to safely use DAP or choose flowering creepers for scorching Indian summers), and on-ground nursery tours in Delhi NCR and across India, we demystify gardening for everyone.

Our Defining Motto

"A one stop shop for Plants, Buildings, plots, flats and interior and exterior."

We believe that a truly happy home combines two things: the right structural space (plots, flats, well-built homes) and the rejuvenating power of plants (living green walls, balcony foliage, and landscape serenity).

Social Cause In Action

1,500+ Free Plants Independence Day Drive

Real environmental activism happens on the ground. During our flagship Independence Day Green Initiative at Balinagar / Ramesh Nagar in Delhi, we personally distributed over 1,500 free saplings to local families, children, and elderly residents.

Each sapling was accompanied by hands-on planting demonstrations, soil-mix guidance, and basic care instruction cards to ensure that the plants didn't just find homes—they flourished.
