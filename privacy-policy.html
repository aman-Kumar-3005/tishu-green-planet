Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Tishu Green Planet, we respect your privacy. When you interact with our website, request a plant or property consultation, or contact us through our web forms, we may collect personal details including your name, email address, phone number, and specifics regarding your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Respond to your questions, nursery tour requests, or property advisory queries.
  • Coordinate WhatsApp communication regarding appointments or quotes.
  • Improve our website performance, content offerings, and user experience.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to third-party marketing companies under any circumstances.

3. Third-Party Services & Embedded Content

Our website includes links to third-party platforms such as YouTube (@TishuGreenplanet), Instagram (@tishugreenplanet), WhatsApp, and Google Maps. These external sites operate under their own independent privacy policies. We encourage you to review their respective policies when visiting their platforms.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our site may utilize basic analytics cookies to understand website traffic patterns, visitor interactions, and popular pages. These cookies do not extract sensitive personal identity data.

5. Data Security & Contact

We take appropriate technical measures to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of your information. If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@tishugreenplanet.com.
