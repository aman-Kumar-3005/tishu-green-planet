Practical Plant Knowledge

Mastering Plant Care in Indian Climates

Tips grounded in trial, error, and thousands of community comments from our YouTube channel.

The DAP Fertilizer Rule

Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP) is widely recommended for root growth, but over-application burns plants within 48 hours. Our golden rule: Only 4–6 granules for a 10-inch pot, strictly away from the central stem, followed by immediate deep watering.

Best time: Early morning or post-sunset during active growth periods.

Beating 45°C Delhi Summers

Peak May–June heat can desiccate tropical plants. Grouping pots together creates a micro-humidity pocket that lowers surrounding temperature by 3–4°C. Mulch topsoil with dried cocopeat or pine bark to prevent rapid moisture loss.

Avoid cold tap water during afternoon peak heat.

Balcony Flowering Creepers

Looking for year-round color? Rangoon Creeper (Madhumalti), Allamanda cathartica, and Bougainvillea spectabilis thrive in full sun and produce cascading floral blankets with minimal organic compost feeding.

Train on wire trellis early for symmetric wall coverage.
